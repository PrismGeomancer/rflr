A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
take a shot.

Which wallets can I connect?

RAFFLER discovers installed Solana wallets through Wallet Standard. Choose your wallet from the picker and approve the connection in your wallet app.

How are my odds calculated?

Your entries divided by the total accepted entries. Before you enter, the estimate includes the entries you are about to add. Your final odds can change as more people enter.

What can I find in a receipt?

The selected wallet, winning entry, total entries, draw time, and reference. Open a receipt to explore its details, or download a copy.

Where are my entries saved?

In this browser, organized by wallet address. Refreshing preserves them, but switching devices does not. Export a backup from My entries to move your history.

What does connecting my wallet allow?

Your wallet shares its public Solana address. RAFFLER can read its SOL balance through your selected RPC. You stay in control of your wallet.
